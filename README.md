## **2. Scripts**

**pEPRI Identification**

*0. Preparation*

1) Remove adapters and PCR duplicates, map reads to the genome using STAR, and remove background (IgG): These steps are performed using the RICpipe (https://github.com/caochch/RICpipe) and CRIC-seq (https://github.com/HuNaijing/CRIC-seq) pipelines.
2) Define enhancer and promoter regions: Enhancer and promoter regions for HeLa cells were defined as previously described (https://github.com/liangliangibp/Alu).

*1. Collect Interaction*

After background removal, inter-molecular chimeric reads are uniquely mapped to enhancers and promoters.

*2. Construct Background*

Monte Carlo simulation is utilized to identify high-confidence pEPRIs.

**Mapping Risk Variants onto pEPRIs**

To estimate the effects of variants on RBP binding, a *k*mer enrichment-based approach was applied. For each variant, a variant-centered *k*mer sequence was generated from the reference genome, and the corresponding alternative *k*mer sequence was generated by introducing the alternative allele. These variant-centered *k*mer sequences were matched against RBP-specific RNA Bind-n-Seq (RBNS) and SELEX datasets, and the corresponding *k*mer enrichment scores were used as proxies for relative RBP-binding affinity. Changes in predicted binding affinity caused by variants were estimated by comparing the enrichment scores between alternative and reference *k*mer sequences (Alt/Ref). Variants showing an 1.5-fold change in predicted binding affinity were selected for subsequent analysis.

Furthermore, knockdown RNA-seq data were incorporated to identify variants with potential regulatory consequences based on additional criteria: the fold change in expression of pEPRI target genes after RBP knockdown had to be greater than 1.5, and the FPKM of target genes had to be greater than 1. Finally, risk variants predicted to alter RBP binding and disrupt pEPRI-associated regulation were identified for downstream analyses.

## **3. Example Input and Output**

Briefly, using HNRNPU CRIC-seq as an example, we selected 1/1,000 of the inter-molecular chimeric reads after background removal and mapped them to enhancers and promoters. Subsequently, Monte Carlo simulation was employed to identify high-confidence pEPRIs (raw *P*-value < 0.05 and support read count > 2). The input and output files for each procedure are provided in this folder.

## **4. pEPRI Network**

This folder contains the high-confidence pEPRI networks for 52 profiled RBPs investigated in our study.
