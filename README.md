## **2. Script** ##

**pEPRI identification**

*0. preparation*

1) Remove adapters and PCR duplicates, STAR map to genome and remove background (IgG): These steps using the RICpipe (https://github.com/caochch/RICpipe) and CRIC-seq (https://github.com/HuNaijing/CRIC-seq) pipelines.
2) Define enhancer and promoter regions: Enhancer and promoter regions for HeLa cells were defined as previously described (https://github.com/liangliangibp/Alu).

*1. collect_interaction*

After removed background, inter-molecular chimeric reads are uniquely mapped to enhancers and promoters.

*2. construct_background*

Using Monte Carlo simulation to identify high-confidence pEPRIs.

**Mapping risk variants onto pEPRIs**

To estimate the effects of variants on RBP binding, a *k*mer enrichment-based approach was applied. For each variant, a variant-centered *k*mer sequence was generated from the reference genome, and the corresponding alternative *k*mer sequence was generated by introducing the alternative allele. The variant-centered *k*mer sequences were matched to RBP-specific RBNS and SELEX datasets, and the corresponding *k*mer enrichment scores were used as proxies for relative RBP-binding affinity. The changes in predicted binding affinity caused by variants were estimated by comparing the enrichment scores between alternative and reference *k*mer sequences (Alt/Ref). Variants showing an absolute log2 fold change ≥ 0.6 (corresponding to a ≥ 1.5-fold change in predicted binding affinity) were selected for subsequent analysis.

Furthermore, knockdown RNA-seq data were incorporated to identify variants with potential regulatory consequences. Additional criteria were applied: the fold change in expression of pEPRI target genes after RBP knockdown had to be greater than 1.5, and the FPKM of pEPRI target genes had to be greater than 1. Finally, risk variants predicted to alter RBP binding and disrupt pEPRI-associated regulation were identified for subsequent analyses.

## **3. Example_input_output** ##

Briefly, we used HNRNPU CRIC-seq as an example and selected 1/1000 inter-molecular chimeric reads of HNRNPU CRIC-seq after removed background, then we mapped these chimeric reads to enhancers and promoters. After that, we used Monte Carlo simulation to identify high-confidence pEPRIs (Raw *P*-value < 0.05 and support read count > 2). And the input and output for each procedures are listed in this folder.

		Cytoscape session displaying the pVTF map for 52 RBPs.
		Two versions of the PTBP1 pVTF map are provided: 
		(1) PTBP1_pVTF_map_no_affinity_cut-off.cys (Figure S6A): No PTBP1 binding affinity fold-change threshold was applied.
